# v024 transport: PLAY toggles the velocity, REC toggles the write bit.
# (v0.21 unified model: no `play` variable, no ST_* states.)
# Free play, 72 BPM. Film log at 2 fps: rows every 500 ms.
# PLAY LED (bit 12) follows velocity != 0; REC LED (bit 13) follows
# rec_write. PLAY must stop a moving tape and start a stopped one;
# REC must arm/disarm without touching the velocity.
T
100 enc 0 6
300 btn 12
1500 btn 12
2500 btn 12
3500 btn 13
4500 btn 13
5500 btn 12
6500 end
